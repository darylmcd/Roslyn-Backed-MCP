**Fixed:** `get_syntax_tree(startLine, endLine)` now collects all top-level statements whose span overlaps the requested line range, not just statements fully contained within it. Previously, a statement that started before `startLine` but ended within the range (or started within the range but ended after `endLine`) was silently dropped. The predicate was changed from `TextSpan.Contains` to `TextSpan.OverlapsWith`. Closes gh #621.
