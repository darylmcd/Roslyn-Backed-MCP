- **Fixed:** Undo claim/commit/clear races and repeat reverts (`undo-unclaim-resurrects-slot-after-clear`, `undo-seq0-history-entry-lost-on-commit-unclaim-race`, `revert-by-sequence-already-reverted-indistinct`): an interrupted `revert_last_apply` / `revert_apply_by_sequence` no longer revives an undo slot or history entry for a workspace closed mid-restore, and a stale commit, `CaptureBeforeApply` or change record that races or follows a close no longer re-creates history, a pending slot or a ledger (`CaptureBeforeApply` on a workspace that closes mid-capture now throws `KeyNotFoundException`; a change recorded for a workspace that is not open is dropped without firing `ChangeRecorded`); a commit racing an unclaim of the sequence-0 slot keeps its history entry. Public behavior change: `revert_apply_by_sequence` on a sequence already reverted this session, or being reverted by a competing call, now returns `reason=already-reverted` with its own message instead of `reason=unknown-sequence` ("from before this session"); clients matching `unknown-sequence` to detect a repeated revert must also handle `already-reverted`, and `unknown-sequence` is now returned only for sequences that never had a revertable snapshot.
