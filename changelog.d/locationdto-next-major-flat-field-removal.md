category: Changed — BREAKING
---
- **Changed — BREAKING:** The flat location members of `SymbolDto`, `DiagnosticDto` and `TypeUsageDto` are removed; `location` is the only representation, and `location.endLine` / `location.endColumn` are now nullable (`locationdto-next-major-flat-field-removal`, `locationdto-partial-span-representation-5-0`; completes ADR 0001 stage 3). Removed from every tool result that returns these DTOs: `filePath`, `startLine`, `startColumn`, `endLine`, `endColumn` on symbols, `startLine`, `startColumn`, `endLine`, `endColumn` on diagnostics, and `filePath`, `startLine`, `startColumn`, `endLine`, `endColumn`, `containingMember`, `previewText`, `classification` on type usages. `location` is now populated whenever the finding has a file and a start position, including a build diagnostic that carries `file(line,column)` with no end (both `endLine` and `endColumn` are then `null` instead of the whole `location` being `null`). `TypeUsageDto.location` is always present. Diagnostics from `analyze_snippet` and `evaluate_csharp` now carry their positions in `location` under the virtual `filePath` `<snippet>` / `<script>`. `DiagnosticDto.filePath` is the one flat member that stays (see `diagnostic-file-only-location-representation`); a diagnostic with a file but no position has `location: null` and keeps `filePath`. The `symbol_search` summary rows keep their compact `filePath` / `startLine` / `startColumn` fields. `PropertyWriteDto` and `MutationCallerDto` are unchanged: their flat fields stay until `locationdto-secondary-dto-flat-field-lifecycle` is decided. Migration: read `location.filePath`, `location.startLine`, `location.startColumn`, `location.endLine` and `location.endColumn` (accept `null` for the two end fields), and for type usages read `location.containingMember`, `location.previewText` and `location.classification` instead of the removed flat members.
